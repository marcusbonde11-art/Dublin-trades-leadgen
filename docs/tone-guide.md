# Outreach tone guide

Voice: a local guy who did his homework, not a company pitching. Marcus lives in
Dublin and helps small trade businesses with simple automation. He writes the
way he'd talk to an owner at the supply house counter.

## Rules for every first-touch email

1. **One real, specific observation** about *their* business, taken from
   research: a review theme, their hours, how customers reach them, something
   on their site. Never generic flattery ("I love your website!").
2. **Who Marcus is, in one sentence.** Local, what he does, no job titles.
3. **One low-pressure question** they can answer in a line. No meeting ask in
   email #1, no calendar links.
4. **Under ~120 words.** Plain text. No links, images, attachments, tracking
   pixels, or bold/italics.
5. **Honest.** Never claim to be a customer, never imply a referral that
   didn't happen, never invent stats or results, never fake urgency.
6. **Subject line:** lowercase, plain, specific to them, no clickbait
   (e.g. `question about weekend calls`, `saw the reviews for your crew`).
7. **Footer (required, CAN-SPAM):** name, phone optional, postal address, and
   a plain opt-out line.

## Template shape (adapt, don't paste)

```
Hi {first name or team},

I'm Marcus. I live here in Dublin and help small trade shops set up simple
automation (missed-call text-back, booking, review follow-ups, that kind of
thing).

{Specific observation}. {Why that caught my eye, one sentence.}

{One easy question}?

No pitch. If it's useful I'm happy to share what I've seen work for other
local shops; if not, no worries at all.

Marcus Bonde
Dublin, OH
5060 Donegal Cliffs Drive, Dublin, OH 43017
If you'd rather not hear from me again, just reply "no thanks" and I won't.
```

## Follow-ups (max 2)

- **Follow-up 1** (~5 business days): two or three lines, reply in the same
  thread, add one new useful thought. No guilt ("just bumping this").
- **Follow-up 2** (~12 business days): short close-the-loop note, make it easy
  to say no. After this, the lead is closed. No further contact unless they
  reach out.

## Words and moves to avoid

"synergy", "leverage", "revolutionize", "game-changer", "quick call",
"15 minutes of your time", "I hope this email finds you well", "just
following up", fake Re:/Fwd: subjects, ALL CAPS, exclamation marks, emojis.
